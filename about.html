About kingpinMAX

What the kit is, and why it's built as one product instead of a catalog.

kingpinMAX makes a kingpin-to-axle measurement reference kit for 53' trailers — a set of high-visibility stickers that mount to the trailer wall and show the kingpin-to-axle distance required under California's rule, the federal cross-border standard, and the marks used in Connecticut, Indiana, Maine, and New York.

The kit ships as a single product, not a catalog by state. A trailer doesn't stay in one jurisdiction — it runs California one week and crosses into Canada or Mexico the next — so the wall carries every mark a route could require, all at once. The driver or inspector reads the one that applies wherever the trailer is standing right now.

What the kit doesn't do

It's a reference, not a certification. The kit does not exempt an operator from complying with local, state, or federal law, and regulations can change — see the full compliance disclaimer for the details.
