KPRA = Kingpin to Center of Rearmost Axle · KCRT = Kingpin to Center of Rear Tandem

The 41′ Marker: North American Standard.

Compliant with US STAA access, Canadian MOU, and Mexican SCT/NOM-012 regulations. This is the default setting for cross-border operations.

States Measuring From Kingpin to Center of Rearmost Axle: CA · CT · IN · ME · NY

All other states measure from kingpin to center of tandem axle assembly.

The Mechanism

KPRA vs KCRT, in one diagram.

Both measurements start at the same kingpin. KPRA runs to the center of the rearmost axle. KCRT stops earlier, at the midpoint between the two tandem axles.

Cross-Border Standard

41' KCRT is the default for USA · Canada · Mexico.

When a route crosses a border, the 41' federal mark (STAA 1982, and the corresponding Canadian and Mexican standards) is the one to check first — it's built into the kit as the cross-border default, with the metric conversion (12.5 m) shown right on the sticker.

State-specific marks (40' California, 43' Northeast) still apply inside their own jurisdiction on top of the federal default.
